# Email templates

Short, plain, owner-to-owner. Send from your own inbox (not a bulk tool), and fill in every
[bracket] before sending. Every email to someone who hasn't bought yet ends with your name,
business name, mailing address, and a way to say "no thanks."

---

## 1. Meeting confirmation (send within 5 minutes of booking)

**Subject:** Our call [Thursday at 4:30] (missed calls)

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the time on the phone. You're on my calendar for **[Thursday, Oct 24 at 4:30pm]**,
> 15 minutes on a video call: [meeting link]
>
> You mentioned [calls going to voicemail while you're on jobs]. I'll show you exactly what your
> callers would get instead, on the real product.
>
> Nothing to prepare. If the time stops working, just reply and we'll move it.
>
> Chris Cardozo
> Callwell · [Your phone]
> [Mailing address]

*Why:* repeating their own problem back reminds them why they said yes, and "nothing to prepare"
removes a reason to cancel.

## 2. Reminder (morning of the meeting)

**Subject:** Today at [4:30]

> Hi [Name], quick reminder about our 15 minutes today at [4:30]: [meeting link]
> Talk soon,
> Chris Cardozo

## 3. No-show (send 15 minutes after the missed start)

**Subject:** Missed you, no problem

> Hi [Name], looks like today got away from you. Happens to everyone in the trades.
> Want to grab [Tuesday at 7:30am] or [Wednesday at 5pm] instead? Just reply with the one that works.
>
> Chris Cardozo · [Your phone]

*Why:* no guilt, two specific times, and a one-word reply to say yes. Try once more after this, then
move them to "Call back" in 60 days.

## 4. After the demo, if they said yes

**Subject:** Getting Callwell set up

> Hi [Name],
>
> Great talking today. Here's your sign-up link for the **[Receptionist]** plan: [link]
>
> Next steps:
> 1. Sign up with the link (about 2 minutes)
> 2. We do a 15-minute setup call to pick your local number and which phone it rings:
>    [day/time]
> 3. Texting goes live once carrier registration clears, usually [__] business days. You're not
>    charged until your line is live.
>
> Any questions, call or text me directly at [Your phone].
>
> Chris Cardozo

Only promise "not charged until live" if you set it up that way (for example, with a free trial
covering the registration wait).

## Cold email (if you email a business before calling)

**Subject:** Missed calls at Callwell

> Hi [Name],
>
> [Personal first line based on something real, like "Saw you offer 24/7 emergency service. That
> must mean a lot of late-night calls."]
>
> Quick question: when you're on a job and can't pick up, what happens to that call?
>
> I'm a local founder in [Ames], and I built a tool that texts those callers back from your business
> number right away, or has an AI receptionist take the job details. You get a text with every new
> lead and reply "1" to call them back. No app to learn, and your phone still rings first.
>
> Worth a 15-minute look? Here's my calendar: [booking link]
>
> Chris Cardozo
> Callwell · [Your phone]
> [Mailing address]
> Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

Follow up once after 3 days ("Bumping this in case it got buried. Worth 15 minutes?") and once
after 7 days. Then stop.
