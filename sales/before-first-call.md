# Before your first call

Everything here is free unless marked $. Work top to bottom; most of it takes an evening.

## 1. Look like a real business (prospects will Google you)

- [x] **Name chosen: Callwell.** Register **getcallwell.com** when you buy a domain (it was free on
      Oct 24, 2026; callwell.com is already registered), and search the name at uspto.gov before
      spending on branding.
- [ ] **Separate business phone number:** a free Google Voice number (voice.google.com) rings your
      cell, keeps your personal number private, and gives you a business voicemail greeting.
- [ ] **Business email:** free for now (e.g. `[name].[business]@gmail.com`).
      $ Upgrade later with a domain (about $10–15 a year) and an address like `you@yourbusiness.com`.
- [ ] **Voicemail greeting** on that number:
      "You've reached Chris Cardozo at Callwell. Leave a message or text this number and I'll get back to you today."
- [ ] **Email signature:** name · business · phone (and mailing address once you have one).

## 2. Make booking effortless

- [ ] **Booking page:** Google Calendar's free "appointment schedule" or a free Calendly account,
      with 15-minute slots at 7–9am and 4–6pm on weekdays.
- [ ] **Video calls:** Google Meet or Zoom (free). Test screen sharing once.
- [ ] Put the booking link into the email templates.

## 3. Get the demo ready

- [ ] Start the app: in the project folder, run `npm run dev`, then open `localhost:3000/demo`.
- [ ] Do a full practice share: Meet or Zoom → share only that browser tab → click through a call, the
      inbox, the reviews, and finish on the **AI** tab ("What it can do" → the "Never" rules).
- [ ] Know the texting pitch by heart: "Miss a call, you get a text. Reply 1 and you're talking to them."
- [ ] Quiet room, earbuds or a headset, laptop plugged in, notifications off.

## 4. Fill in the placeholders

- [ ] **Pitch deck:** your name, phone, the founding-offer limit and deadline, and a referral reward (or delete that line).
- [ ] **Email templates:** name, business name, phone, booking link.
- [ ] **Website** (`src/lib/site.ts`): contact email; mailing address once you have one.
- [ ] **Mailing address:** required in every cold *email* (not for calls). $ A P.O. box is about
      $30–80 for six months. Skip cold email until you have one, and stick to calls.

## 5. Know your honest answers

Practice these out loud. Owners will ask.

| They ask | You say |
|---|---|
| "How long have you been around?" | "I'm just launching. You'd be one of my first ten businesses, which is why I set it up with you personally." |
| "Who else uses it?" | "Nobody yet. I'm looking for a few founding customers in [Ames] to start with." |
| "What if it breaks?" | "Your phone still rings first. If my system has a problem, calls go straight to you like today." |
| "Can I try it first?" | "Yes, there's a [14]-day free trial and no contract." |
| "Do the Google reviews work?" | "That part is launching soon; I'm waiting on Google's approval. Text-back and the receptionist work now." |
| "Where are you located?" | "[Ames]. I'm local." |
| "How do I know what the AI says?" | "Every call has a transcript, and there's a page that shows the exact rules it follows and everything it's allowed to do. It never quotes prices or promises times." |
| "Can you text me a demo?" | Only once your line is live. Until then: "Let me show you on screen what the texts look like." (Texting slide + the demo's AI tab.) |

## 6. Practice before real calls

- [ ] Read the call script out loud 5 times.
- [ ] Role-play 3 calls with someone playing a busy, skeptical plumber (a friend or family member,
      or ask Claude to play one).
- [ ] Record one practice call on your phone and listen back: slow down, cut filler words.

## 7. First calling session

- [ ] Open `Call Tracker - Ames.xlsx`. Block 7:00–8:30am on a weekday.
- [ ] Start with the top 10 rows. Before each call, glance at the business's Google listing for a
      detail to mention.
- [ ] Log every call right away. Add "Do not call" requests to that tab the same day.
- [ ] After 10 calls, write down which question got owners talking, and lead with it next time.

## Free help nearby

Iowa's Small Business Development Center (America's SBDC Iowa) is based at Iowa State in Ames and
offers free one-on-one advising on registering your business name, taxes, and pricing. Worth one
meeting before your first sale.
